# Isopod-X Toy Line — Series 1 Factions

Series 1 is organized into three factions with four figures each: **Earth, Air, and Water**. The roster intentionally stays within the world of insects, arthropods, and insect-sized creatures so the entire line feels like one coherent micro-war universe.

The main selection rule is **silhouette diversity**. Every figure should remain recognizable as a blacked-out shape from across a room, with a body plan, weapon language, and toy profile that feels distinct from the rest of the line.

## Earth Faction

Earth characters should feel heavy, grounded, armored, subterranean, and difficult to move.

| Character | Animal | Silhouette Hook | Combat Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Isopod-X** | Isopod | Broad overlapping shell, compact body, huge boots, antennae | Armored hero / all-rounder |
| **War Wētā** | Wētā | Huge head, crushing jaws, massive spiked legs | Heavy bruiser |
| **Pincer Punk** | Earwig | Narrow body with enormous rear forceps | Grappler |
| **Velvet Vengeance** | Velvet ant | Compact fuzzy body, narrow waist, heavy sting apparatus | Berserker |

### Earth Visual Direction

- **Isopod-X** is the visual anchor of the line: broad, rounded, armored, and compact.
- **War Wētā** should read almost gorilla-like, with oversized thighs, feet, and jaws.
- **Pincer Punk** should be taller and narrower, with giant rear forceps rising into the silhouette like twin weapons.
- **Velvet Vengeance** should contrast soft/fuzzy sculpted texture against the hard shells of the rest of the faction.

## Air Faction

Air characters should emphasize speed, height, wings, blades, and piercing attacks. Their silhouettes should generally feel more vertical and angular than Earth.

| Character | Animal | Silhouette Hook | Combat Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Razor Prayer** | Praying mantis | Tall frame, tiny waist, enormous folded blade arms | Duelist |
| **Needle Strike** | Assassin bug | Narrow angular body, long piercing rostrum | Assassin |
| **Static Screamer** | Cicada | Huge domed eyes and broad roof-like wings | Sonic artillery |
| **Jawstorm** | Dobsonfly | Giant curved mandibles and large swept wings | Airborne heavy |

### Air Visual Direction

- **Razor Prayer** should be one of the tallest figures in Series 1, with oversized mantis blades defining the entire silhouette.
- **Needle Strike** should be lean and angular, with the proboscis reading almost like a built-in bayonet.
- **Static Screamer** should have broad folded wing panels and oversized eyes, giving the figure a dome-like upper silhouette.
- **Jawstorm** should use a male dobsonfly's giant mandibles as the primary visual feature, creating an aggressive flying-jaws profile.

## Water Faction

Water should contain some of the strangest anatomy in the line. Aquatic arthropods and insect-sized creatures allow silhouettes that feel alien even next to the Earth and Air factions.

| Character | Animal | Silhouette Hook | Combat Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spectrum Smasher** | Mantis shrimp | Giant hammer fists and stalked eyes | Boxer / pile driver |
| **Siphon Strike** | Water scorpion | Long body, grasping forelegs, extended breathing siphon | Spear fighter |
| **Triops Tyrant** | Tadpole shrimp / Triops | Huge shield-like carapace and forked tail | Ancient tank |
| **Depth Charger** | Predaceous diving beetle | Smooth shell and enormous paddle-like rear limbs | Aquatic speedster |

### Water Visual Direction

- **Spectrum Smasher** should exaggerate the striking clubs until they are nearly torso-sized.
- **Siphon Strike** should be long and spear-like, with the breathing siphon functioning visually like a lance or tail weapon.
- **Triops Tyrant** should look prehistoric, with much of the upper body hidden beneath a broad semicircular shield shell.
- **Depth Charger** should be the cleanest, most streamlined silhouette in the line, with oversized swimming paddles replacing ordinary-looking rear limbs.

## Silhouette Summary

The Series 1 figures should be immediately distinguishable:

- Isopod-X = armored ball
- War Wētā = gorilla-like jaw bruiser
- Pincer Punk = tall rear-pincer fighter
- Velvet Vengeance = compact fuzzy berserker
- Razor Prayer = blade tower
- Needle Strike = needle / bayonet silhouette
- Static Screamer = winged dome
- Jawstorm = flying jaws
- Spectrum Smasher = giant fists
- Siphon Strike = spear body
- Triops Tyrant = walking shield
- Depth Charger = torpedo / paddle-limbed speedster

## Design Principle

Series 1 should feel like a box of tiny biological war machines rather than generic animal-men. Creature anatomy should drive armor, weapons, articulation, and personality. Whenever possible, a figure's natural body plan should become its primary toy gimmick rather than simply giving every character conventional weapons and human proportions.
