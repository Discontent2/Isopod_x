# Game 1 Boss Roster

Eight stage bosses are selected from the Series 1 micro-beast toy roster. Isopod-X is the player character and is not counted as a boss.

| Stage | Boss | Animal | Element(s) | Fight identity |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pincer Punk | Earwig | Earth | Grappler; giant rear-forceps attacks |
| 2 | Razor Prayer | Praying mantis | Air | Fast blade duelist; jumping and aerial slashes |
| 3 | Spectrum Smasher | Mantis shrimp | Water | Heavy puncher; shockwave strikes and armored counters |
| 4 | Velvet Vengeance | Velvet ant | Earth + Air | Aggressive stinger; burrowing rushes and leap attacks |
| 5 | Triops Tyrant | Triops / tadpole shrimp | Water + Earth | Ancient armored tank; shell defense and mud-floor attacks |
| 6 | Jawstorm | Dobsonfly | Air + Water | Flying mandible predator with aquatic-larval influence |
| 7 | War Wētā | Wētā | Earth | Heavy bruiser; stomps, jaws and arena destruction |
| 8 | Siphon Strike | Water scorpion | Water + Air | Spear-like forelegs and breathing-siphon weapon; final stage boss |

## Element balance

Each element appears exactly four times across the eight bosses:

- Earth: Pincer Punk, Velvet Vengeance, Triops Tyrant, War Wētā
- Air: Razor Prayer, Velvet Vengeance, Jawstorm, Siphon Strike
- Water: Spectrum Smasher, Triops Tyrant, Jawstorm, Siphon Strike

## Reserved Series 1 characters

Needle Strike, Static Screamer, and Depth Charger remain available for minibosses, secret encounters, rival roles, later games, or other content.
