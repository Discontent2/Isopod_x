# ISOPOD_X: QUEST FOR THE SHELL CORE
## Issue #0: THE SHELL YOU CHOOSE

This is an 8-page prologue comic designed to establish X, the Springtail Peashooter, his idea of home, the Shell Core crisis, and the eight-stage quest without trying to retell the entire game.

## COVER 1: FRONT COVER

**ISOPOD_X**  
**QUEST FOR THE SHELL CORE**

Subtitle:

**THE SHELL YOU CHOOSE**

No dialogue. X stands with the living Springtail Peashooter latched to one arm, purple Essence glowing from its muzzle. Behind him, the three worlds of **Earth, Air, and Water** converge around a distant fractured Shell Core.

## COVER 2: INSIDE FRONT

Small opening text:

> Every creature is born inside a world it thinks is everything.
>
> X learned otherwise.

Credits and title can eventually occupy the rest of the page.

# PAGE 1

### PANEL 1
A peaceful forest floor at insect scale.

Moss towers like trees. Water beads cling to fungi. Fern roots curve overhead. Tiny organisms move through a rich, enormous micro-world.

X is much less armored here, wandering through leaf litter. He is unmistakably himself, broad shell, antennae, claws, oversized personality packed into a tiny body.

**CAPTION:**  
Before the armor. Before the Core. Before anyone called him X...

### PANEL 2
X pauses beside a drop of water and drinks.

A tiny **springtail** watches him from a mushroom cap.

X notices it.

**X:**  
You following me?

The springtail tilts its head.

### PANEL 3
The forest suddenly goes silent.

X's antennae rise.

The springtail freezes.

Something enormous shifts behind the leaves.

**SFX:**  
KRRRSSSH...

### PANEL 4
A gigantic natural predator erupts from the vegetation.

X immediately curls.

Not fear.

Instinct.

Armor plates lock together.

### PANEL 5
X becomes a compact armored ball and **ROLLS**, smashing beneath the predator and launching dirt and pebbles everywhere.

The springtail leaps aboard.

**X:**  
Hold on.

# PAGE 2

### PANEL 1
X barrels through the undergrowth with the predator behind him.

Then suddenly...

DAYLIGHT.

An enormous human hand enters the world from above.

### PANEL 2
The fingers close around X.

The scale difference should feel almost cosmic.

X claws uselessly at the thumb.

**CAPTION:**  
Then the sky grew fingers.

### PANEL 3
X is dropped into a battered white Styrofoam container.

Inside are frightened beetles, worms, springtails and miscellaneous captive invertebrates.

The lid closes overhead.

**SFX:**  
THUNK.

Darkness.

### PANEL 4
X slams against the container wall.

Nothing.

Again.

Nothing.

The tiny springtail from earlier lands beside him.

### PANEL 5
X sits against the wall.

For the first time, he looks defeated.

The springtail nudges one of his claws.

**X:**  
Don't look at me like that.

Beat.

**X:**  
I'm thinking.

# PAGE 3

### PANEL 1
Night.

The container sits among equipment in a pet shop back room or improvised specimen area.

Through a crack, strange purple light pulses from discarded equipment outside.

Inside, X has organized the other prisoners.

Tiny creatures have chewed, scratched and weakened one corner of the container.

**X:**  
Again.

### PANEL 2
X charges.

**SFX:**  
KRACK!

The weakened wall breaks.

### PANEL 3
Chaos.

X and the tiny creatures spill onto the workbench.

A container of glowing purple material tips during the escape.

Purple **Essence** splashes across the surface.

The springtail lands in it.

### PANEL 4
The little creature arches as Essence moves through its translucent body.

Its anatomy shifts subtly.

Its front becomes a narrow organic firing aperture.

Its legs become powerful gripping hooks.

It is still clearly alive.

Still clearly a springtail.

Just... different.

X stares.

### PANEL 5
A pursuing predator lunges onto the table.

The springtail leaps onto X's forearm and locks itself in place.

**CHK-CHK.**

X looks at it.

The creature looks forward.

**X:**  
You sure about this?

Tiny chirp.

**SPRINGTAIL:**  
*ktik.*

### PANEL 6
X raises his arm.

A violent purple Essence shot erupts from the living weapon.

**SFX:**  
KRAK-KZZZT!

**X:**  
Peashooter it is.

# PAGE 4

### FULL-WIDTH OPENING PANEL
**YEARS LATER.**

X's home.

A tranquil chamber built inside architecture resembling the interior of an enormous isopod carapace.

Ribbed shell forms curve overhead. Organic structures blend with old machinery. Soft blue and violet lights glow between the plates.

Nothing is grotesque. It feels protective.

Safe.

### PANEL 2
X sleeps heavily in his recessed bed.

Boots on the floor.

Armor partly removed.

For once, no battle.

### PANEL 3
Across the room, a rugged computer sits on a desk.

Its screen suddenly activates.

**SFX:**  
BEEP.

**COMPUTER:**  
CORE NETWORK FAILURE.

### PANEL 4
X's eyes open.

### PANEL 5
He sits at the computer.

On the screen are three symbols:

**EARTH. AIR. WATER.**

Beneath them:

**8 RELAYS LOST**

### PANEL 6
X looks toward the weapon display.

Only one weapon waits there.

Peashooter.

It presses its tiny face against the glass.

**X:**  
Yeah.

**X:**  
I saw it.

# PAGE 5

### PANEL 1
X opens the display case.

Peashooter immediately crawls up his arm and locks onto his forearm.

**SFX:**  
TK-TK-TK-CHK.

### PANEL 2
X finishes fastening his blue armor.

Red X chest plate.

Massive boots.

Silver shell.

The transformation is visual rather than magical.

The wandering creature from Page 1 is now the warrior we know.

### PANEL 3
The computer displays the eight hostile signatures.

Their portraits appear mostly in shadow:

**PINCER PUNK**  
**RAZOR PRAYER**  
**SPECTRUM SMASHER**  
**VELVET VENGEANCE**  
**TRIOPS TYRANT**  
**JAWSTORM**  
**WAR WĒTĀ**  
**SIPHON STRIKE**

### PANEL 4
Three branching routes spread across the monitor.

Earth tunnels.

Air structures.

Water chambers.

At their center, a fractured purple symbol:

**THE SHELL CORE**

**COMPUTER:**  
Core stability: 31%.

### PANEL 5
X stands.

Peashooter's purple core begins glowing.

**X:**  
Eight thieves.

Beat.

**X:**  
One Core.

### PANEL 6
X heads for the door.

**X:**  
That's almost convenient.

# PAGE 6

### PANEL 1
The shell-like entrance to X's home opens.

Beyond it lies a vast underground Earth-sector tunnel.

### PANEL 2
X steps through.

His computer map appears briefly on a wrist display.

The first target flashes:

**PINCER PUNK**

### PANEL 3
A huge pair of forceps slices through a fungus stalk in front of X.

**SNAP!**

### PANEL 4
PINCER PUNK emerges.

An armored earwig warrior.

Short, broad torso.

Massive rear forceps curve around his body like twin weapons.

Distinct purple and brass armor.

He grins.

**PINCER PUNK:**  
Little shell wandered into the wrong dirt.

### PANEL 5
X looks at him.

Then at Peashooter.

Then back at him.

**X:**  
I've had worse landlords.

# PAGE 7

### PANEL 1
Pincer Punk attacks.

His forceps crash together where X stood.

**SFX:**  
KLANG!

### PANEL 2
X rolls underneath him.

A true armored wheel.

Shell plates visibly rotating.

### PANEL 3
Pincer Punk catches X mid-roll between the enormous pincers.

X strains as they squeeze.

**PINCER PUNK:**  
Earth belongs to the strong!

### PANEL 4
Close on X.

Not panicked.

Annoyed.

**X:**  
That's what big things always say.

### PANEL 5
Peashooter twists independently on X's trapped arm.

Its purple core brightens.

Pincer Punk notices.

Too late.

### PANEL 6
Point-blank Essence blast.

**SFX:**  
KZZZ-THOOM!

The forceps spring apart.

# PAGE 8

### PANEL 1
Pincer Punk crashes through a pile of rotten wood.

X lands in a crouch.

Smoke curls from Peashooter's little mouth.

### PANEL 2
Pincer Punk tries to rise.

X places one massive boot on one of his forceps.

Not killing him.

Ending the fight.

**X:**  
Where's the relay?

### PANEL 3
Pincer Punk points reluctantly toward an ancient mechanism embedded in the tunnel wall.

A fragment of purple Shell Core energy flickers inside.

### PANEL 4
X restores the relay.

A pulse of violet light races through the roots and underground structures.

On his wrist display:

**CORE STABILITY: 39%**

One boss portrait goes dark.

Seven remain.

### PANEL 5
Montage strip.

Razor Prayer opens enormous mantis blades.

Spectrum Smasher raises hammer-like fists beneath water.

Velvet Vengeance turns toward the camera.

Triops Tyrant rises beneath an ancient shell.

Jawstorm spreads monstrous mandibles.

War Wētā cracks his knuckles.

Siphon Strike waits motionless in black water.

### PANEL 6
Back at the tunnel entrance.

X looks toward the enormous world ahead.

Peashooter crawls onto his shoulder for a moment.

X scratches beneath its chin with one claw.

**X:**  
Seven more.

Peashooter chirps.

**SPRINGTAIL:**  
*ktik.*

X smiles.

**X:**  
Yeah.

**X:**  
Then we go home.

**CAPTION:**  
HOME ISN'T THE SHELL YOU'RE GIVEN.

**CAPTION:**  
IT'S THE ONE YOU FIGHT YOUR WAY BACK TO.
